# Economic research

**What it is.** Framing a narrow economic question, identifying the mechanism and counterfactual, specifying evidence before analysis, testing the claim against competing explanations, and turning the result into a decision-useful paper.

**What I can do with it.** This capability is being developed through the individual research paper. The brief, spec, dated drafts, figures, final paper, and prompt log together show the research process.

AI may support source discovery, explanation, figure-building from a human-written specification, and critique. The research brief, specification, paper prose, analysis, recommendation, and reflection remain human-authored.
